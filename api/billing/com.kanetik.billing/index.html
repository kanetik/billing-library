RevocationReason
Link copied to clipboard
enum RevocationReason : Enum<RevocationReason>

Why a purchase was revoked, as decoded by the consumer from the originating transport (RTDN payload, server reconciliation result, support-tool action, etc.). The library does not validate the mapping — the enum exists to give downstream collectors a typed switch for differentiated UX (e.g. a chargeback might warrant a security-flag entry while a refund warrants a neutral "we've reversed your purchase" notice).
